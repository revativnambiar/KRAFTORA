Our Core Philosophy

Why Only One Bag?

Fast-fashion and conventional retail rely on infinite variants that encourage overconsumption. At KRAFTORA, we set out with a different aim: design a single, perfected everyday shopping bag that solves the moisture issue inherent to traditional paper bags.

By combining natural kraft paper with a tailored removable insert made from discarded umbrella and raincoat panels, we created an enduring carry system that keeps single-use plastic out of the grocery run while giving existing materials a second chapter.

Core Innovation

The Removable Navy Liner

Unlike standard coated bags where plastic lamination is permanently bonded to paper fibers — making recycling impossible — our liner remains completely separate.

Fold-Over Rim Security: The liner's top 2–3 cm edge folds gently over the bag's upper rim to stay locked in position during packing.
Zero Adhesive Construction: Not a drop of glue connects the fabric to the kraft paper, preserving both material streams.
Quick-Lift Cleaning: Lift out the pouch anytime to shake out grocery crumbs or wipe clean with a cold damp cloth.
Endless Portability: If your paper bag ever suffers structural fatigue, simply move the navy liner to any new grocery kraft bag.
