The Splash-Resistant Reusable Shopping Bag combines two purposeful materials: a natural kraft paper outer bag and a removable inner liner stitched from reclaimed umbrella or raincoat fabric.


The navy liner sits neatly inside the bag and folds over the top rim to stay secure during shopping. It is not glued in — it lifts out in a single motion for cleaning or when the paper bag needs to be recycled separately.


The liner provides splash-resistance against damp grocery items and rainy days. It is not designed to be submerged as fully waterproof. The outer kraft paper bag remains completely uncoated and unlaminated throughout.
