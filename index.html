Material Responsibility

Two Materials.
One Enduring Solution.

Kraft paper shopping bags are beloved because they are renewable, biodegradable, and feel natural to hold. Yet their primary vulnerability has always been moisture: a single leaking carton can weaken paper fibers.

At the same time, thousands of umbrellas and raincoats are discarded every season simply due to broken spokes or torn zippers — while their hydrophobic fabric panels remain in prime condition.

We unite these complementary materials. Our dark navy-blue reclaimed liner acts as an unglued splash shield, protecting the paper exterior while keeping materials completely separable at end-of-life.
