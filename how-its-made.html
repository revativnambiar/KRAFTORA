Transparent Craftsmanship

The Making Process

A 5-step handcrafted process that pairs a clean kraft paper shopping bag with reclaimed umbrella fabric to create an everyday splash-resistant carry solution.

Step 1 — Collect

Gather Structural Kraft Bags

We select clean, dry, undamaged kraft paper grocery bags with intact rectangular bases and strong rope handles. The bag's interior dimensions (Length, Width, Height) serve as the exact measurement blueprint for the custom liner.

Step 2 — Source

Select Reclaimed Fabric

We salvage post-consumer umbrellas and raincoats that are no longer usable due to broken ribs or torn hardware. The fabric panels are thoroughly inspected, washed, and sorted. We specifically harvest dark navy-blue panels for high density and water-shedding performance.

Step 3 — Measure & Cut

Measure & Cut 5 Custom Panels

Based on the bag's interior cavity, we cut five distinct fabric pieces: one base panel (L × W), two long side panels (L × H), and two short side panels (W × H). A 1–1.5 cm seam allowance is included along each joining edge to guarantee a neat, snug interior fit.

Step 4 — Stitch

Stitch & Assemble Liner Pouch

The five panels are joined into an open-top box pouch using heavy-duty straight stitching. Corner seams are reinforced to carry heavy grocery weights without separating. The top edge is folded to produce a clean, smooth rim tuck.

Step 5 — Fit & Test

Fit Inside Bag & Splash-Test

The stitched liner is placed inside the kraft paper bag and folded 2–3 cm over the rim. We test that the liner lifts out effortlessly and run a droplet splash test to ensure moisture beads off the liner without seeping through to the paper shell.
