Our Origin & Purpose

A Simple Idea.
Thoughtfully Executed.

KRAFTORA started with an honest observation: kraft paper shopping bags are practical, biodegradable, and comfortable to carry — but vulnerable to moisture. Discarded umbrellas and raincoats, meanwhile, offer durable water-resistant fabric that often ends up in landfills.

The Concept

Two Components.
Zero Virgin Plastic.

Conventional "eco-bags" often replace paper with thick non-woven polypropylene plastic, which requires hundreds of uses to break even in environmental footprint. Other manufacturers laminate plastic films directly onto paper, ruining paper recyclability forever.

At KRAFTORA, we chose a completely different approach. We keep the outer bag purely unbleached, uncoated kraft paper. We stitch a separate, tailored inner liner from clean, reclaimed umbrella or raincoat cloth.

The liner folds neatly over the rim to lock in place during use, repelling moisture from damp vegetables or cold milk. When you return home, lift the liner out to shake or rinse clean. If the paper bag eventually wears out, the liner transfers into a fresh kraft bag.

Guiding Principles

Principles Behind the Craft

We hold our design to realistic everyday utility and honest environmental stewardship.

100% Upcycled Liner Cloth

Every liner is handcrafted from reclaimed umbrellas or raincoats. Zero new polyester or nylon is manufactured for our products.

True Curbside Recyclability

Because we never laminate or glue the liner to the paper, the outer bag recycles in standard paper recycling streams without contamination.

Transparent Performance

We do not claim our bag is submersible or fully waterproof. It is splash-resistant against drizzle and damp grocery condensation.

Extended Material Lifecycle

The fabric liner is designed to outlive dozens of paper shells. Move it seamlessly to your next kraft bag whenever needed.

Ergonomic Daily Comfort

Reinforced twisted cord handles and a sturdy gusset base allow the bag to stand upright on supermarket checkout counters.

Zero Exaggerated Claims

No invented certificates or inflated eco-metrics. Real craftsmanship, durable materials, and thoughtful design speak for themselves.
